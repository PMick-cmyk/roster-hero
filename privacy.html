Privacy Policy

Roster Hero · Last updated 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

Roster Hero (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is provided by Roster Hero, ABN 36 119 705 048, Australia. You can contact us at pekinmichael@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

The app helps public health nurses and midwives in South Australia read a roster screenshot, estimate pay, test shift swaps and, if you choose, add shifts to a calendar and check them against your other events.

3. Information we handle and where it goes

Roster and screenshots. When you choose a roster screenshot, the text is read on your own device. The image is not uploaded to us. The dates and shift codes you confirm are saved in your browser’s storage on your device.

Pay settings. Your pay point, allowances, deductions (including any salary sacrifice amounts you enter) and change history are saved on your device. They are not sent to us.

Google Calendar (only if you connect it). When you tap “Connect Google Calendar”, Google asks you to approve access. The app then works directly between your browser and Google:

  • It reads your list of calendars, and the times, titles and all-day status of events in the dates around your roster, and whether you declined an event, so it can warn you about clashes.
  • It creates, updates or removes calendar events that the app itself created for your shifts. It does not change other events.
  • It asks Google for these permissions: view and edit events on your calendars, and view your calendar list.
  • Your Google sign-in token is kept in your browser’s memory for the session. We do not receive, copy or store your calendar data.

Backup files. If you download a backup, the file is created on your device and stays with you. We never receive it.

Support messages. If you contact us (for example with the “Report a problem” button), we receive your email address, your message and the technical details in the email, such as the app version and your device and browser type. We receive your roster and settings only if you choose to include them, and never any dollar amounts.

Payments. The app is currently free to use. If paid plans are introduced, payments will be handled by a payment provider and we will update this policy before you pay. We will not see or store your card number.

Technical information from other providers. To work, your browser downloads code and fonts from third parties, including Google Fonts, a code delivery network (for the text-reading engine and its language data) and Google (for sign-in). These providers can see your IP address and device details when your browser contacts them, under their own policies. The app also stores its own files on your device so it can open offline.

4. Sensitive information

A work roster, pay rate and salary packaging details are personal and private, and a roster can reveal where you work. We do not collect health information about you or about patients. Please do not put patient information in any message to us.

5. How we use information

We use the information above only to provide the features you use, to respond to your support requests and to keep the app secure. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence models.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Roster Hero’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the calendar features that are visible in the app, and we do not transfer it to others, use it for advertising, or allow people to read it, except where you ask us to, where it is needed for security or to comply with the law.

7. Storage, security and retention

Roster and settings data sits in your browser on your device, so its security depends partly on your device (use a screen lock). We cannot recover it if you clear your browser data, so the app offers a backup file. Support emails are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Use “Delete all my data from this device” on the Help page, or clear the app’s data in your browser settings.
  • Use “Disconnect” on the Calendar page, and remove the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Calendar events the app added to your calendar stay until you delete them.
  • To ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to have support emails deleted, contact pekinmichael@gmail.com.

9. Overseas providers

Some providers named above are based overseas, including in the United States. Your browser contacts them directly.

10. Children

The app is intended for adults who work in health rostering and is not directed at children.

11. Complaints

Contact us first at pekinmichael@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

12. Automated processing

The app calculates pay estimates and flags roster issues using fixed rules. We do not make decisions about you that affect your rights or interests; you decide what to do with the estimates.

13. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes and tell you in the app before we use your information in a new way.
